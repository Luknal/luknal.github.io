Blendshape weights

VRM exposes five mouth shapes. Weights are sharpened and normalised before they are applied — summing them raw produces one averaged half-open mouth, which is the single most common lip-sync bug.

The four modules under src/lipsync/ are copied byte-for-byte from the production application — no demo-only reimplementation. Design notes and write-up
