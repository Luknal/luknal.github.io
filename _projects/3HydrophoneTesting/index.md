## Overview

Tested ultrasound transducers with hydrophone in Averkiou's Lab. The transducer and hydrophone are both submerged in a glass tank. A motorized positioner mounted on an aluminum frame above the tank moves the hydrophone, and controlled it from LabVIEW to get measurements like pressure field, gain, focus distance, and central frequency.
